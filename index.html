FAQ

Everything about Paird: pairs, payouts, launching, the board and $PAIRD.

Basics

What is Paird?

A way to launch a coin on StonkFun that's paired with something real, a stock, a pre-IPO name or another StonkFun coin, and pays its holders in that asset. Pair with NVIDIA and your holders get paid in NVIDIA.

Why "Paird"?

Paired and paid. Your coin is paired with an asset, and your holders get paid in it.

Is Paird live? Can I pair a coin today?

The site and the live board are up; pairing isn't yet. It opens with $PAIRD. Until then you can browse every StonkFun coin, every pair, and work out payouts with real volume.

Which chain is it on?

Solana. Coins launch on StonkFun, which runs on Raydium LaunchLab.

Is Paird part of StonkFun?

No. Paird is independent and not affiliated with StonkFun, Raydium, Jupiter or any company a coin is paired with. The board reads StonkFun's public API, charts come from DEX Screener and swaps run through Jupiter.

Pairs

What does "paired with" mean?

Every StonkFun coin's curve is priced in a quote asset. Most use SOL; many use tokenized stocks like NVDAx or TSLAx, pre-IPO names like OpenAI, or other coins. That asset is the pair. Your coin trades against it, so every buy of your coin buys the pair first.

What can I pair with?

Anything StonkFun lists as a quote: tokenized stocks, pre-IPO names, baskets, currencies, SOL and other coins. Most paird on the home page shows what coins are paired with right now, from StonkFun's API.

What does "avoid the PvP" mean?

When a coin runs, copies launch against it and split the buyers. Pair with the coin instead: your coin trades against it, buys of yours buy it first, and your holders get paid in it. You ride its narrative instead of fighting it.

Can I change the pair later?

No. The pair is set at launch, the same as on StonkFun.

Payouts

Where do the payouts come from?

A coin's creator fees. Every trade pays a creator fee; 90% goes to the payouts you choose and 10% to Paird.

What payouts can I choose?

Holders (split across holders by bag), The Drop (one holder wins the whole pot each round), Burn (buys your coin back and burns it), You (to the creator) and Team (up to five wallets). Mix them in any split up to 90%.

What is paid out?

The pair. A coin paired with NVDAx pays NVDAx; a coin paired with SOL pays SOL.

How often are payouts sent?

Every round, once the pot is worth sending, so dust doesn't eat it in network fees.

How is The Drop drawn?

From a recent Solana blockhash and the holder list at that block, weighted by bag. Anyone can recompute it.

How does the payout check work?

Pick a live StonkFun coin and it starts from that coin's real 24h volume and its reported fee. Then it shows what 90% of those fees would pay all holders a day, your share for the bag you set, and your odds in each Drop.

Launching

How will pairing a coin work?

Name, ticker, image, the pair, your payout split and your socials. You sign once and the coin, its pool and its payout rules go live in the same transaction.

When does it open?

With $PAIRD. Follow Paird on 𝕏 for the date.

What does the ticker check do?

Type a ticker and Paird searches StonkFun and Jupiter for coins with that exact ticker, so you know if it's taken before you launch.

The board

Where does the data come from?

Coins, pairs, market caps and volume come from StonkFun's public API. Holders and price moves come from Jupiter. Nothing on the board is simulated.

How often does it update?

New coins every 10 seconds, volume and market caps every 45 to 60 seconds, holders every 40 seconds.

How do I trade a coin?

Open any coin and use the trade box. Swaps run through Jupiter and you sign them in your own wallet. Paird never holds your funds.

Where is my watchlist saved?

Only in your browser.
